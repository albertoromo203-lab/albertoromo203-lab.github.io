Learn Mandarin with us

Academia Potochino is an online Mandarin academy for Mexican students who already speak English and are learning Chinese as their third language.

What we share

On Instagram and TikTok we post short carousels about the Chinese language and culture: tones, characters, everyday phrases, studying in China and the HSK exams. Every post is written and reviewed by the academy.

Potochino Poster

Potochino Poster is the academy's internal publishing tool. It takes carousels that the academy has approved in its content calendar and sends them to the academy's own TikTok account, where a team member adds a sound and publishes them. It is not offered to other users and does not post to anyone else's account.

Read the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy for how it uses TikTok.
