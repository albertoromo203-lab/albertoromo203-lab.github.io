Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information Academia Potochino ("we", "us") handles through this website and through Potochino Poster, our internal tool that publishes the academy's approved carousels to its own TikTok account.

Information we receive from TikTok

When the academy's account owner connects TikTok, TikTok gives the tool:

  • an access token and a refresh token, which let the tool act for that account;
  • the account's open ID, and its basic profile information (display name and avatar), from the user.info.basic permission;
  • the status of each post the tool sends, from the video.upload or video.publish permission.

How we use it

Only to send the academy's approved carousels to the academy's own TikTok account and to check whether each one arrived. We do not use it for advertising or profiling, and we do not sell or share it with anyone.

Where it is kept

Tokens are stored privately on the academy's own computer. Post records (title, caption, slides and status) are kept in the academy's private content calendar in Notion.

How long we keep it

Tokens are kept until the access is revoked or the tool is retired, and are then deleted. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services permissions.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or forms. It loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
